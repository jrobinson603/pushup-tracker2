Training notes

Form & recovery: Stop sets before form breaks down. Rest 60–120 seconds between sets, take the two scheduled recovery days, and stop if you experience sharp or persistent pain.

Quick warm-up

Before each workout: arm circles for 30 seconds, shoulder rolls, 10 wall push-ups, then 5 easy push-ups if comfortable. Afterwards, gently stretch your chest, shoulders, and triceps.

The goal is ambitious; repeat a week or reduce reps if recovery or form deteriorates. Don't max out every day.
